I want support planning the tasks and milestones around a transition.  When I create a transition I want an LLM to ask me questions to size the scope and scale of the transition.  I want the LLM to provide a recommended set of tasks, milestones and appropriate activities to ensure timely transition. Transitions types are: Contract, Personnel, System. Creating a transition should initiate an interim planning step that creates the appropriate tasks with details and sets/assigns the tasks to appropriate user team roles. This step could be direct to an LLM or be an iniated N8N workflow.
